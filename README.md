# 🍕 Pizza Sales Performance Dashboard

<img src="pizza-dashboard.png" width="100%">

## 📊 Project Overview
This dynamic and interactive Excel dashboard was built from scratch to analyze and visualize sales data for a pizza restaurant. The primary goal of this project is to track key performance indicators (KPIs), understand customer purchasing behavior, identify top-selling products, and pinpoint peak ordering hours to support operational and marketing decisions.

---

## 💡 Key Insights & Analytics
* **Total Sales:** Achieved a total revenue of **$4,004.50** across **3,550** total units sold.
* **Top-Selling Categories:** **Classic** and **Chicken** pizza categories dominated the sales, contributing the highest volume and revenue.
* **Preferred Pizza Size:** **Large (L)** size was the absolute customer favorite, accounting for over **39%** of the total sales volume.
* **Hourly Order Trends:** The fluid trendline graph highlights a clear surge in order volumes during lunch and late dinner hours, specifically peaking between **5:00 PM and 7:00 PM**.

---

## 🛠️ Excel Tools & Features Used
* **Data Cleansing:** Prepared raw data and handled date/time formats accurately for seamless analysis.
* **Pivot Tables:** Aggregated and summarized complex rows of data to extract vital business metrics.
* **Advanced Charts:** Leveraged doughnut charts, horizontal bar graphs, and smooth area/line trendlines for strong data visualization.
* **Dynamic Slicers & Timelines:** Integrated bi-directional interactive filters (Pizza Category, Size, and Time) to provide a highly flexible reporting experience.
